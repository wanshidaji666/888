Hastelloy C-276 Plate (UNS N10276)

The benchmark nickel-chromium-molybdenum plate for the most aggressive chemical environments — wet chlorine, hypochlorite and chlorinated acids — supplied to ASTM/ASME SB-575 (2.4819).

Ni-Cr-Mo Alloy Plate

The last line of defense against aggressive media

When no other alloy survives, C-276 usually does. Its high molybdenum (15–17%) and tungsten (3–4.5%) content with a low-carbon, controlled-silicon chemistry delivers exceptional resistance to wet chlorine gas, hypochlorite, chloride-bearing oxidizing acids and hot contaminated sulfuric acid. The low carbon content means welds do not sensitize, so fabrications keep their corrosion resistance in the as-welded condition — no post-weld heat treatment required.

ALLOYS supplies C-276 plate in the solution-annealed condition with full heat traceability, ASTM G28 intergranular corrosion testing on request and cut-to-size processing to your drawings.

Applications

Where C-276 plate performs

💧 Wet Chlorine & Hypochlorite

Chlor-alkali cells, bleach plants and chlorine-dioxide washers — environments that destroy most metals within weeks.

🧪 Acetic & Chlorinated Acids

Reactors, columns and heat exchangers handling acetic acid, mixed chlorinated organics, wet hydrogen chloride and contaminated sulfuric acid.

🏭 Flue Gas Scrubbers

FGD inlet ducting, dampers and scrubber internals in power and incineration plants, where chlorides and SO2 condense at temperature.

🛢️ Sour Oil & Gas

Downhole and process equipment exposed to high-chloride, sour brines at elevated temperature, plus pulp-and-paper bleach-plant hardware.
