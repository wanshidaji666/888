Alloy 625 Plate (UNS N06625)

A nickel-chromium-molybdenum plate combining outstanding corrosion resistance with high strength from cryogenic to 980 °C — supplied to ASTM/ASME SB-443 and EN 10095 (2.4856).

Ni-Cr-Mo Superalloy Plate

Strength and corrosion resistance in one grade

Alloy 625 earns its place wherever stainless steel cannot survive: the solid-solution strengthening from niobium and molybdenum delivers yield strength above 414 MPa in the annealed condition, while the high chromium and molybdenum content resists pitting, crevice corrosion and oxidizing media. Its exceptional fabricability means it welds without post-weld heat treatment and retains toughness at cryogenic temperatures.

ALLOYS stocks and rolls Alloy 625 plate in both Grade 1 (annealed) and Grade 2 (solution treated) conditions, with full heat traceability and cut-to-size processing to your drawings.

Applications

Where Alloy 625 plate performs

✈️ Aerospace Engine Parts

Exhaust systems, thrust-reverser components, fuel and hydraulic line hardware, and turbine shrouds that must hold strength across a wide temperature range.

⚗️ Chemical Processing

Reactors, heat-exchanger components and piping exposed to oxidizing and reducing acids, wet chlorine and hot contaminated media.

🌊 Seawater Service

Offshore components, propeller blades and subsea hardware where high strength meets resistance to pitting and crevice attack in stagnant or flowing seawater.

⚛️ Nuclear Industry

Reactor-core and control-rod components, and steam-generator hardware valued for corrosion resistance and toughness after long service exposure.
