Buyer FAQ

Common questions from importers and distributors.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Stock sizes: no MOQ, even one bundle. Custom production runs start from 25 MT per item. Mixed containers (multiple products in one 20GP/40HQ) are welcome.

What are your payment terms?

30% T/T deposit + 70% against B/L copy is standard. L/C at sight is accepted for orders above 100 MT. Flexible terms for repeat buyers after the first two orders.

Can you supply third-party inspection?

Yes. We work with SGS, BV and TÜV. Inspection can be arranged before loading at buyer's cost; in-house QC reports and mill test certificates (EN 10204 3.1) are free with every shipment.

Which trade terms do you quote?

FOB (Shanghai / Ningbo / Qingdao) by default. We also quote CFR and CIF to any major port worldwide, and EXW for buyers with their own forwarders in China.

What is the typical lead time?

Stock items: 5–7 days to port. Production orders: 15–25 days depending on tonnage and rolling schedule, plus ocean transit.

Can you do OEM / custom branding?

Yes — custom bundle tags, stencilled marks, private-label packaging and paper tube printing for coils are all available at no extra cost for orders above 50 MT.

Do you provide samples?

Free samples (0.5–2 kg) of coil, wire and bar products are available; international courier freight is collected. Structural sections can be inspected via video call of stock yard.
