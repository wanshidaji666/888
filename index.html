FAQ

Questions that affect specification and landed cost

Do I need A588, A242 or an EN grade?

If your project follows US standards, A588 is the default structural choice and A242 is common for architectural work. European projects usually specify S355J0WP or S355J2W. Send us the drawing or standard reference and we will confirm the match.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Plate: trial orders from 5–10 tons are workable. Coil: normally 25 tons per size. Cut-to-size parts can be smaller — we quote per drawing.

Will Corten steel continue to rust and stain surroundings?

The patina stabilizes after the first wet–dry cycles. On facades we recommend pre-weathered sheet or a sealing rinse, plus detailing that keeps runoff away from concrete — covered in our patina article.

How is the plate packed for export?

Plates are stacked on steel skids, wrapped in waterproof paper and edge-protected, then steel-strapped and loaded with rust-preventive desiccant. Container loading photos are provided for every shipment.

Can you supply mill test certificates?

Yes — EN 10204 3.1 MTC is standard on every heat, covering chemistry and mechanical properties. Third-party inspection (SGS / BV / TUV) can be arranged before loading.

What are typical lead times?

Stock sizes: 7–15 days to port. Rolling to order: 20–35 days depending on thickness and volume. Cut-to-size adds 3–7 days. We confirm the exact date in the quotation.
