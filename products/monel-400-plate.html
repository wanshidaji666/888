Monel 400 Plate (UNS N04400)

A nickel-copper plate with excellent corrosion resistance in seawater, hydrofluoric acid and reducing media — supplied to ASTM/ASME SB-127 and EN 10095 (2.4360).

Ni-Cu Alloy Plate

The workhorse for seawater and reducing media

Monel 400 is a solid-solution binary nickel-copper alloy — roughly two-thirds nickel, one-third copper — that resists flowing seawater, hydrofluoric acid, alkalis and many reducing chemicals better than virtually any commercial alloy at comparable cost. It retains ductility and toughness from sub-zero temperatures up to about 480 °C, and work-hardens readily for higher-strength hardware.

ALLOYS supplies Monel 400 plate in hot-rolled and annealed conditions, with full heat traceability, 100% ultrasonic examination on request and cut-to-size processing to your drawings.

Applications

Where Monel 400 plate performs

🌊 Seawater Condensers & Pumps

Power-station condenser tube sheets, seawater pump bodies, impellers and valves where flowing seawater would rapidly attack other alloys.

🧪 Hydrofluoric Acid

HF acid alkylation units, pickling equipment and fluorine-handling vessels — Monel 400 is the reference material for anhydrous and aqueous HF.

⚓ Marine Hardware

Shipbuilding fasteners, propeller shafts, pump shafts and splash-zone cladding combining strength with dependable corrosion resistance in salt spray and immersion.

🏭 Chemical & Hydrocarbon Processing

Crude distillation columns, heat exchangers and vessels handling alkalis, salt brines and reducing acids free of oxidizing contaminants.
