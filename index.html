FAQ

Questions that affect specification and landed cost

Which grade do I need for my service conditions?

Send us the media, temperature and pressure. As a starting point: seawater and HF acid → Monel 400; sulfuric/phosphoric acid → Incoloy 825; wet chlorine and the most aggressive acids → C-276; high temperature plus corrosion → Alloy 625.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Plate from stock: single sheets are workable. Mill rolling to order: normally 500 kg–2 t per size depending on grade. Cut-to-size parts can be smaller — we quote per drawing.

Are Inconel, Monel, Incoloy and Hastelloy your products?

They are registered trademarks of Special Metals and Haynes International. We supply the equivalent generic grades — UNS N06625, N04400, N08825 and N10276 — produced to the same ASTM/ASME specifications, which is what fabricators and inspection bodies actually order against.

Why do nickel alloy prices change so often?

Nickel, molybdenum and chromium are exchange-traded metals; mill prices follow the LME and spot alloy markets. Quotations are typically valid 3–7 days, and long-term projects often fix prices with a surcharge mechanism.

What tests come with the plate?

Every heat ships with chemistry and mechanical test results per EN 10204 3.1. We can add intergranular corrosion (ASTM G28), ultrasonic examination (ASTM A578) and third-party inspection (SGS / BV / TUV) on request.

What are typical lead times?

Stock sizes: 7–15 days to port. Mill rolling: 45–70 days depending on grade and volume. Cut-to-size adds 3–7 days. We confirm the exact date in the quotation.
