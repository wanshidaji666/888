ASTM A242 Type 1 Weathering Steel Plate

High-strength low-alloy weathering steel plate favored by architects for its fine, even patina — the classic Corten grade for facades, sculptures and lightweight structures.

Architectural Grade

The original Corten chemistry

ASTM A242 was the first weathering steel standard — the steel that gave Corten its name. Type 1 chemistry, with phosphorus controlled at the upper end, produces a particularly fine-grained, uniform oxide layer that architects value on visible surfaces: the patina darkens evenly and does not streak as coarsely as structural grades.

SHANDONG DAZHI supplies A242 Type 1 plate from 1.5 to 50 mm, either as full mill plates or cut-to-size panels for cladding packages. For facade work we can also pre-weather the surface so panels match from the day they are installed.

Applications

Where A242 Type 1 plate is specified

Rainscreen & Cladding Panels

Solid weathering steel panels and cassette systems where the patina is the finished surface — no paint, no coating maintenance cycle.

Sculpture & Public Art

The fine, even oxide layer of Type 1 makes it the preferred grade for large-format sculptures and land-art installations.

Lightweight Structures

Pergolas, shade canopies, signage frames and landscape features built from thinner gauge plate for weight-sensitive designs.
