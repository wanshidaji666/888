Corten Steel Coil & Sheet

Hot-rolled weathering steel coil, slit strip and flat sheet in ASTM and EN grades — plus a pre-weathered sheet option for facade packages that must match from day one.

Volume Supply

Coil, slit strip and sheet from one mill

Where plate serves the fabricator, coil and sheet serve the rollformer, the panel maker and the continuous production line. SHANDONG DAZHI hot-rolls weathering steel coil to ASTM A588, A242 and EN 10025-5 chemistry in thicknesses from 1.5 to 12.7 mm, widths up to 2,350 mm and coil weights up to 35 tons.

Coils can be supplied as-cut or processed in-house: slit into narrow strip for profiling lines, leveled and cut to flat sheet, or accelerated-weathered so the oxide layer is already stable before fabrication. Pre-weathered sheet eliminates the patchwork appearance of freshly mixed panels on a facade.

Applications

Where corten coil and sheet are used

Facade & Cladding Panels

Rollformed standing-seam and cassette profiles from slit strip — or flat sheet for CNC-cut panel packages on rainscreen systems.

Roofing & Architectural Metal

Corten sheet and strip for roofing profiles, gutters, flashings and cornices that weather to a uniform chocolate-brown finish.

Wagons & Trailers

Coil feed for wagon side panels, hoppers and container components where abrasion plus corrosion resistance is required.

Pre-Weathered Panel Packages

Accelerated patina applied in the mill so delivered sheets already show the stable final color — no streaking, no waiting for weather cycles on site.
