ASTM A588 Corten Steel Plate

High-strength low-alloy structural weathering steel plate in Grade A and Grade B, rolled for welded, bolted and riveted construction where atmospheric corrosion resistance is required.

Best Seller

Structural weathering plate, Grade A & B

ASTM A588 covers high-strength low-alloy structural steel with an atmospheric corrosion resistance up to four times that of carbon structural steel. The alloying elements — copper, chromium and nickel — form a dense, adherent oxide patina that seals the surface and slows further corrosion, eliminating the need for paint in most exposed structural applications.

SHANDONG DAZHI rolls A588 plate from 1.5 to 100 mm in both Grade A and Grade B. Grade A is the workhorse for bridge girders and welded structures; Grade B suits heavier sections and higher service temperatures. Every heat is tested for chemistry and mechanical properties before release.

Applications

Where A588 plate is specified

Bridge & Highway Structures

Girders, cross-frames and box sections for unpainted weathering steel bridges — the largest single use of A588 plate worldwide.

Buildings & Facade Substructure

Exposed columns, canopies, bracing and support frames where the structural frame stays visible and paint-free by design.

Rail Cars & Freight Wagons

Wagon bodies and hoppers combining 50 ksi strength with corrosion resistance against cargo residue and weather exposure.
