Incoloy 825 Plate (UNS N08825)

A nickel-iron-chromium plate stabilized with titanium for outstanding resistance to sulfuric and phosphoric acids, stress-corrosion cracking and pitting — supplied to ASTM/ASME SB-424 (2.4858).

Ni-Fe-Cr Alloy Plate

Built for acid service at a sensible cost

Incoloy 825 sits in the sweet spot between stainless steel and full nickel alloys: enough nickel (38–46%) to resist chloride stress-corrosion cracking, molybdenum and copper for reducing acids, and a deliberate titanium addition that stabilizes the grade against intergranular attack after welding. The result is a plate that handles sulfuric and phosphoric acid across a wide concentration and temperature range at a lower cost than Ni-Cr-Mo grades.

ALLOYS supplies Incoloy 825 plate in the annealed condition with full heat traceability, intergranular corrosion testing (ASTM G28) on request and cut-to-size processing to your drawings.

Applications

Where Incoloy 825 plate performs

🧪 Sulfuric & Phosphoric Acid

Acid plants, pickling tanks, heat exchangers and piping carrying sulfuric and phosphoric acids across wide concentration and temperature windows.

🏭 Pollution Control (FGD)

Flue gas desulfurization scrubber components, dampers and ductwork exposed to wet sulfur dioxide and chloride-bearing condensates.

🛢️ Oil & Gas Downhole

Sour-service downhole tubulars, tool joints and wellhead hardware where chloride stress-corrosion cracking rules out standard stainless steels.

⚗️ Chemical Processing

Reactors, evaporators and tank linings for mixed acetic, formic and nitric acid streams, plus nuclear fuel reprocessing equipment.
